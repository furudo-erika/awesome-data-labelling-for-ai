## Why Data Labelling for AI Matters

Different AI systems need very different labels. An LLM needs expert-written demonstrations and preference rankings; a vision model needs pixel-accurate masks; an autonomous vehicle needs labelled 3D point clouds. The best data labelling for AI is the provider or tool that matches your modality and quality bar.

## Selection Criteria

Every entry in this list of best data labelling for ai was assessed against these criteria:

- Modality fit (text/LLM, image, video, 3D, audio, medical)
- AI-assisted pre-labelling to reduce cost
- Human expertise where models are weak
- Measurable label quality and feedback loops
- Integration with ML pipelines and data storage

## FAQ

### Why is data labelling important for AI?

Supervised and post-trained AI models learn from labelled examples. Label quality directly limits model accuracy, safety, and reliability.

### Can AI label data for AI?

Partly. Foundation models such as SAM or GPT-class LLMs can pre-label data, but humans still review, correct, and handle edge cases, especially for evaluation sets.

### What data labelling does an LLM need?

Typically SFT demonstrations, preference rankings for RLHF or DPO, rubric-based grading for evaluations, and red-teaming prompts for safety.

### How much labelled data do I need?

It depends on the task. Fine-tuning an LLM can work with thousands of high-quality examples; training a vision model from scratch may need hundreds of thousands.
